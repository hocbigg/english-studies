Great Readings

This document consists of great books or articles on English Studies. Some are here because there is a course covering the same material; some are just great books that you should read at some point in your career.

Once you have made it through most of the curriculum, knowing whether a book is worth your time will become easier. Or, if you are struggling in one of the courses, perhaps reading a book on the subject will help.

Name Author(s)
A Glossary of Literary Terms M.H. Abrams and Geoffrey Galt Harpham
Literary Theory: An Introduction Terry Eagleton
How to Read Literature Like a Professor Thomas C. Foster
The Elements of Style William Strunk Jr. and E.B. White
The Western Canon: The Books and School of the Ages Harold Bloom
Writing and Literature: Composition as Inquiry, Learning, Thinking, and Communication (free alternative) Tanya Long Bennett
The Open Anthology of Earlier American Literature (free alternative) Timothy Robbins et al.
