# Advanced Topics in English Studies

This document provides advanced specialization tracks for learners who have completed the foundational undergraduate curriculum in English Studies. Each track reflects upper-division undergraduate seminars and graduate-level methodologies.

### Specialization Tracks

- [Advanced Critical Theory & Cultural Studies](#advanced-critical-theory--cultural-studies): Interrogate power, subjectivity, ideology, and environmental crises through contemporary critical philosophies.
- [Period Specializations & Global Anglophone Literatures](#period-specializations--global-anglophone-literatures): Conduct deep historical, formal, and archival analyses of specific literary epochs and diasporic traditions.
- [Digital Humanities, Book History & Textual Scholarship](#digital-humanities-book-history--textual-scholarship): Combine computational text analysis, TEI encoding, and the material history of print and manuscript culture.
- [Advanced Creative Writing, Poetics & Craft](#advanced-creative-writing-poetics--craft): Master advanced narrative architecture, prosodic theory, dramatic structures, and the lyric essay.
- [Rhetoric, Stylistics & Language Studies](#rhetoric-stylistics--language-studies): Analyze the linguistic mechanics of literature, cognitive metaphor, argumentation, and discourse structures.
