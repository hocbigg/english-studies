# Advanced Topics in English Studies

This document provides advanced specialization tracks for learners who have completed the foundational undergraduate curriculum in English Studies. Each track reflects upper-division undergraduate seminars and graduate-level methodologies.

### Specialization Tracks

- [Advanced Critical Theory & Cultural Studies](#advanced-critical-theory--cultural-studies): Interrogate power, subjectivity, ideology, and environmental crises through contemporary critical philosophies.
- [Period Specializations & Global Anglophone Literatures](#period-specializations--global-anglophone-literatures): Conduct deep historical, formal, and archival analyses of specific literary epochs and diasporic traditions.
- [Digital Humanities, Book History & Textual Scholarship](#digital-humanities-book-history--textual-scholarship): Combine computational text analysis, TEI encoding, and the material history of print and manuscript culture.
- [Advanced Creative Writing, Poetics & Craft](#advanced-creative-writing-poetics--craft): Master advanced narrative architecture, prosodic theory, dramatic structures, and the lyric essay.
- [Rhetoric, Stylistics & Language Studies](#rhetoric-stylistics--language-studies): Analyze the linguistic mechanics of literature, cognitive metaphor, argumentation, and discourse structures.

## Advanced Critical Theory & Cultural Studies

Investigate how literature interacts with social power, philosophy, ecology, and identity through contemporary theoretical frameworks.

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Postcolonial & Decolonial Theory | Analyzes how imperial conquest, colonial discourse, and resistance shape world literatures, language dominance, and hybrid cultural identities. | - Edward W. Said, *Culture and Imperialism* (Vintage)<br>- Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak, "Can the Subaltern Speak?" (Colonial Discourse and Post-Colonial Theory)<br>- Homi K. Bhabha, *The Location of Culture* (Routledge)<br>- Frantz Fanon, *The Wretched of the Earth* (trans. Richard Philcox) |
| Feminist, Queer & Trans Studies | Deconstructs essentialist models of gender, heteronormative social structures, and the performative nature of sexual and bodily identities in literature. | - Judith Butler, *Gender Trouble: Feminism and the Subversion of Identity* (Routledge)<br>- Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick, *Epistemology of the Closet* (UC Press)<br>- bell hooks, *Feminist Theory: From Margin to Center* (South End Press)<br>- Jack Halberstam, *The Queer Art of Failure* (Duke University Press) |
| Critical Race Theory & Black Aesthetics | Examines the construction of race, racialized literary canons, vernacular traditions, and systemic white supremacy across literary forms. | - Henry Louis Gates Jr., *The Signifying Monkey: A Theory of African-American Literary Criticism* (Oxford UP)<br>- Toni Morrison, *Playing in the Dark: Whiteness and the Literary Imagination* (Harvard UP)<br>- Fred Moten, *In the Break: The Aesthetics of the Black Radical Tradition* (Univ. of Minnesota Press)<br>- [MIT OCW: Ethnic Literature in America](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-709-ethnic-literature-in-america-spring-2017/) |
| Ecocriticism & Environmental Humanities | Explores how narrative represents nonhuman agency, ecological collapse, extractive capitalism, and environmental racism. | - Lawrence Buell, *The Environmental Imagination* (Harvard UP)<br>- Rob Nixon, *Slow Violence and the Environmentalism of the Poor* (Harvard UP)<br>- Donna J. Haraway, *Staying with the Trouble: Making Kin in the Chthulucene* (Duke UP) |
| Biopolitics, Affect Theory & New Materialism | Investigates the visceral, pre-linguistic forces of emotion, bodily governance, sovereign power, and the agency of non-human matter. | - Michel Foucault, *The History of Sexuality, Volume 1: An Introduction* (Vintage)<br>- Lauren Berlant, *Cruel Optimism* (Duke UP)<br>- Jane Bennett, *Vibrant Matter: A Political Ecology of Things* (Duke UP) |

## Period Specializations & Global Anglophone Literatures

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |

| Victorian Literature, Imperialism & Industrial Culture | Analyzes the aesthetic responses to industrialization, class formation, evolutionary anxiety, and global British imperial expansion. | - Isobel Armstrong, *Victorian Poetry: Poetry, Poetics and Politics* (Routledge)<br>- Elaine Showalter, *A Literature of Their Own: British Women Novelists from Brontë to Lessing* (Princeton UP)<br>- Catherine Gallagher, *The Industrial Reformation of English Fiction* (Univ. of Chicago Press) |

## Digital Humanities, Book History & Textual Scholarship

Bridge literary criticism with computational analytics, text encoding standards, archival science, and print culture history.

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computational Literary Studies & Distant Reading | Leverages statistical modeling, corpus linguistics, and macroanalysis to study literary shifts across thousands of digitized texts. | - Franco Moretti, *Distant Reading* (Verso)<br>- Ted Underwood, *Distant Horizons: Digital Evidence and Literary Change* (Univ. of Chicago Press)<br>- [The Programming Historian](https://programminghistorian.org/) (Open-access peer-reviewed digital humanities methods) |
| Text Encoding & Digital Scholarly Editing | Teaches XML/TEI standards and digital editorial theory to transcribe, annotate, and preserve primary manuscript and print witnesses. | - [TEI by Example (TBE)](http://teibyexample.org/) (Interactive Text Encoding Initiative tutorials)<br>- Elena Pierazzo, *Digital Scholarly Editing: Theories, Models and Methods* (Open Book Publishers)<br>- Lou Burnard, *What is the Text Encoding Initiative?* (OpenEdition) |
| History of the Book & Material Bibliography | Examines the physical production, circulation, censorship, and reception of manuscripts, printed books, and early periodicals. | - Robert Darnton, "What is the History of Books?" (*Daedalus*, Vol. 111, No. 3)<br>- D. F. McKenzie, *Bibliography and the Sociology of Texts* (Cambridge UP)<br>- David Finkelstein and Alistair McCleery, *An Introduction to Book History* (Routledge) |

## Rhetoric, Stylistics & Language Studies

Analyze how syntactic choices, cognitive metaphors, and rhetorical structures produce literary aesthetics and ideological persuasion.

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classical & Contemporary Rhetorical Theory | Studies persuasive speech, forensic and deliberative argumentation, tropes, figures, and Kenneth Burke's dramatistic pentad. | - Aristotle, *On Rhetoric: A Theory of Civic Discourse* (trans. George A. Kennedy, Oxford UP)<br>- Kenneth Burke, *A Rhetoric of Motives* (Univ. of California Press)<br>- Wayne C. Booth, *The Rhetoric of Fiction* (Univ. of Chicago Press) |
| Literary Stylistics & Foregrounding | Uses linguistic methodologies to objectively analyze authorial style, deviation, defamiliarization, and syntactic choices in prose and verse. | - Geoffrey Leech and Michael Short, *Style in Fiction: A Linguistic Introduction to English Fictional Prose* (Routledge)<br>- Paul Simpson, *Stylistics: A Resource Book for Students* (Routledge English Language Introductions)<br>- Elena Semino and Mick Short, *Corpus Stylistics: Speech, Writing and Thought Presentation in a Corpus of English Writing* (Routledge) |
| Cognitive Poetics & Conceptual Metaphor | Applies cognitive linguistics, conceptual blending, and schema theory to understand how the human mind processes literary texts. | - George Lakoff and Mark Turner, *More than Cool Reason: A Field Guide to Poetic Metaphor* (Univ. of Chicago Press)<br>- Peter Stockwell, *Cognitive Poetics: An Introduction* (Routledge)<br>- Mark Turner, *The Literary Mind: The Origins of Thought and Language* (Oxford UP) |
| Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA) | Exposes how language, grammar, and institutional discourse encode, maintain, and reproduce power hierarchies and social inequality. | - Norman Fairclough, *Critical Discourse Analysis: The Critical Study of Language* (Routledge)<br>- James Paul Gee, *An Introduction to Discourse Analysis: Theory and Method* (Routledge)<br>- Teun A. van Dijk, *Discourse and Power* (Palgrave Macmillan) |
